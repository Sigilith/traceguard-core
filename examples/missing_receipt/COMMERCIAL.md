# Paid implementation offer — proposed service scope

**Make an agent's failure and restart behavior inspectable before connecting it to consequential work.**

ThreeLineage can offer a bounded engagement around one agreed tool or workflow. The public demonstration is the inspection material; the paid work is adapting, integrating, testing and supporting a customer's actual execution boundary.

## Initial engagement

Inputs: one workflow, access to a sandbox, documented policy ownership, and agreed acceptance criteria.

Deliverables:

- Map of policy decisions, side effects and receipt storage.
- Integration of the agreed execution gate around one selected operation.
- Successful, denied, interrupted and restart scenarios with captured evidence.
- Written limitations and recovery procedure specific to the integration.
- Handover documentation and an agreed support period.

Acceptance: the customer can reproduce the agreed scenarios, inspect their effects and evidence, and demonstrate the documented recovery procedure in the sandbox.

Out of scope unless separately agreed: regulatory certification, legal compliance opinions, formal verification, production penetration testing, guarantees of exactly-once external execution and unrestricted autonomous production actions.

## Follow-on work

Additional connector integrations, provider-specific idempotency and receipt checks, independent review support, deployment hardening and ongoing maintenance can be scoped separately.

No market rate or price is asserted here. Quote after confirming the system, risk, integration effort, access and acceptance criteria. A price or deadline in a signed proposal should refer to that bounded scope. This document makes no contract or payment commitment.
